# Project overview
Softorio Workspace is a self-hosted personal and professional operating system for an owner and a small team. It combines personal productivity, career development, knowledge management, business operations and technical asset management. An individual can maintain private personal records and join multiple shared business workspaces. Initial release is internal, invitation-only, with no billing or public registration.
## Objectives
- A daily command center for tasks, schedules, goals, habits and focused work.
- Career roadmaps, courses, certifications, job applications and language learning.
- Private second brain: notes, snippets, prompts, research, bookmarks and reflections.
- Team collaboration, projects, attendance, work reports and approvals.
- CRM, internal finance ledger, software and infrastructure inventories.
- Free-first integrations and optional local AI; core app works without AI.
## Success criteria
Owner can plan a day, connect tasks to goals, track learning, assign and review team work, and retrieve knowledge. Employees cannot retrieve private or unauthorized workspace data. Mobile and desktop usable; backup restoration tested before launch. No additional required subscription, subject to existing hosting and service quotas.
## Non-goals
Native apps, paid WhatsApp automation, regulated accounting, payroll tax compliance, public SaaS billing, microservices and invasive employee surveillance. Future SaaS expansion requires separate approval.
