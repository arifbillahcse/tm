# Phase 11: Team management
Business workspace only. Membership uses existing invitations and roles; no automatic account creation. Owner > admin > manager > member. All active members can view the directory (name, designation, department, responsibilities) and announcements. Owner/admin manage profiles and departments; admins cannot manage peers or owner. Supervisors may review, approve leave and assign onboarding only to lower-ranked active members. Managers supervise members across this workspace (not department-specific). Members see their own attendance, leave, reports, reviews, onboarding and documents; supervisors see lower ranks; owner sees historical records including removed members. Documents: owner/admin upload/remove for self or lower ranks; managers and members download their own documents only. No self-approval or self-review. Attendance uses explicit server UTC timestamps, permits multiple non-overlapping shifts, no covert monitoring. Open shifts require manual checkout. Leave dates use submitter timezone, pending/approved overlaps rejected; no leave balance or payroll calculation. Daily reports are one editable record per employee/local date. Performance reviews use manual 1–5 ratings. Onboarding items support completion/reopening. Announcements are visible within workspace. PDF/PNG/JPEG/TXT private uploads up to 5 MB, 20 per employee. No malware scanner or filesystem/database atomicity guarantee. No external integrations, production deployment, browser or concurrency load verification. Department rename/delete, attendance corrections, leave cancellation, report revision history and announcement editing are not included.
