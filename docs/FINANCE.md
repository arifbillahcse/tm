 # Phase 14 finance
Personal workspace owner only; business owner/admin only, including payroll. Ledger is cash based, not double-entry or tax accounting. Amounts are integer minor units; fixed BDT/USD account currency, separate reporting, manually stored reference exchange rates (no automatic conversions). Opening balances excluded from income. Transactions append-only, ordinary corrections via one opposite-direction reversal on or after original date; linked invoice/payroll payments cannot be reversed in this release. Account balances may be negative. Recurring daily/weekly/monthly entries run via finance:tick, bounded 30 occurrences per rule/run, original monthly day retained, disabled on creator authorization loss, unique occurrence; requires deployed scheduler. Budgets reflect expense reversals; savings progress manually recorded. Payroll snapshots base/bonus/deduction/net; one record per employee/month; full payment once in matching currency. Invoices snapshot customer name, one total, full payment once; no bank movement, payment gateway, tax, partial payments, or invoice line items. CRM deal values and project expense records are separate and not auto-posted. Receipts private PDF/PNG/JPEG/TXT <=5 MB, max20 per entry, no malware scan. CSV monthly ledger export neutralizes spreadsheet formulas; no payroll export. No account/category editing, payment correction UI, inter-account transfers, rate conversion, automatic bank reconciliation, receipt deletion or regulated payroll compliance. Browser/print/accessibility/load and penetration tests outstanding. No deployment.
