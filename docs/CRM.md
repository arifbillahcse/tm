# Phase 13: CRM and sales
Business workspace only; owner/admin/manager have shared CRM access, ordinary members have none. Assignment is organizational, not a record-level ACL. All foreign references resolve inside current workspace; writes serialize with membership changes through the workspace lock. Companies and contacts support edits, segmentation and filtering. Leads record source, assignee and status; only qualified leads convert, once per lead. Deals have a fixed currency, decimal amounts, versioned changes and immutable closed states; winning marks contact customer. Follow-ups are manually scheduled/completed/reopened; no automatic reminders yet. Proposals and quotations are plain-text drafts, printable in browser, with fixed deal currency and manual sent/accepted/rejected/void status. No email delivery, electronic signature, public quote link, PDF engine or invoice/payment integration. Interactions record manual notes, email, phone, meetings, WhatsApp and other communication; no external messages sent. Analytics group amounts by currency and stage, not received revenue. Lead-source counts and due follow-ups are workspace-scoped. Audit records metadata only. No record deletion, import/export, custom pipeline stages, document editing/version history or browser-tested print layout yet. Select lists load all workspace options; larger datasets need searchable pagination. Browser, accessibility, load and penetration testing remain outstanding.
